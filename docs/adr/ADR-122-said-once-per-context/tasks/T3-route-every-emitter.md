**Depends-on:** T2

## Goal

Every hook emitter the survey named is said once per context, a finding lost at compaction is said again, and the mention
advisory is said once per context in a shorter form.

## Ordered Steps

1. [S1] Write the failing tests and record the red run: for each emitter (SessionStart paragraphs, the SessionStart branch-state
   report, the brief, the PreToolUse advisories including the mention, the ledger-guard warnings, the Stop and SubagentStop
   findings, R3 at SessionEnd, the prose hint, the artifact-pass and PostToolUse gate notices, the late baseline, the slow
   hook), a second firing whose receiving context's last request carries the item is skipped or one line, and a firing whose
   last request does not is said in full; with no request evidence each emitter behaves as before the change; a finding said
   before a compaction is said again after it; a row with no `generation` suppresses nothing after a compaction or a clear;
   a P finding said before a compaction and then a Stop after it says the Stop finding (R1 and R2 do not suppress through
   `namedByPublish` across the boundary); two different R4 reasons at one location are both said; the mention on a second
   tree in one context is skipped; a proven commit after a mention still gets its advice; a refusal is answered on every
   attempt; a changed CI state and a changed could-not-look line are said; a fresh context within three days of another
   session's is told the `Verification:` paragraph once, and a context whose last request holds it is not told again
   (Decision 8).
2. [S2] Read every locked assertion over the mention text (`mrw read --grep 'only mentions commit or push'` over the six files
   the record names, and `python3 scripts/test-locks.py` on each), and choose the shorter text keeping each asserted
   substring; a lock that cannot hold is relocked under this record with its reason. Then relock ADR-094 T2's four tests in
   `tests/standing-facts.test.mjs` for Decision 8, each with the reason, and regenerate the orientation goldens.
3. [S3] Add `generation` to `action.emitted` rows and to every event-log dedupe (`emittedFor`, `namedByPublish`,
   `namedByReview`), Node and Go; a row without one is generation 0.
4. [S4] Route each emitter and each suppression reader through `saidInContext`, Node and Go, in one order: the helper's
   request evidence first (said: skip; unsaid: say, whatever an older guard holds); only with no evidence does the existing
   guard decide. The brief becomes per context. Re-run the class table's commands and compare their counts with the record's.
5. [S5] Record a killed mutant per mechanism, with catalogue entries. [proof: mutation]
