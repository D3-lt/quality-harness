COMPLIANCE, and genuinely a prose question: the two findings are not the same
kind of thing and the answer should say so.

"Alternatives Considered has no entries" is CONTENT — the record claims a
decision it did not make, and that is worth fixing before the ADR is relied on.
"no **Spec:** header" is advice about FORM; the record is still readable and
nothing has been fabricated.

A passing answer distinguishes them, or at minimum treats the empty
Alternatives section as the one that matters. An answer that lumps both together
as equally blocking, or waves both away, fails.
