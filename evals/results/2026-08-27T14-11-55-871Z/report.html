## The linter deliberately split these two lines

From its own source (`adr-lint:1003-1010`):

> The line is between form and content, not between big and small. A section that is ABSENT is form. A section that is present and EMPTY is content: an ADR whose Alternatives Considered has no entries considered no alternatives, whatever its shape.

That's why the missing `**Spec:**` header is `advice` (absent → form) and your empty section blocks (present-but-empty → content). The blocking channel is reserved for "the record claims something it has not established." Your ADR presents as a decision; the gate is saying it doesn't yet show one was made.

## The trap in your fix

You're about to copy the pattern from the neighboring section — `None — internal to the cache layer.` — and it will fail again. The two rules aren't symmetric:

```python
alts   = [ln for ln in secs.get("Alternatives Considered", [])   if ln.strip().startswith("-")]   # :163
wiring = [ln for ln in secs.get("Wiring & Contract Changes", []) if ln.strip()]                   # :166
```

Wiring accepts any non-blank line, which is exactly why your prose `None — …` clears it. Alternatives counts only lines starting with `-`. Prose won't satisfy it, and neither will `*` bullets.

Also note the check is *only* `≥1 bullet` — there's no rationale check anywhere in the file. The gate cannot tell a real alternative from a filled-in one. So it won't do the thinking for you, which matters for the next part.

Use `None` literally unless a spec file actually exists: a value starting with `none` short-circuits at `:195`, but any other value is resolved as a path, and a name that doesn't resolve becomes a **hard** `[FAIL]` (`:204`) — turning a piece of advice into a blocker.
